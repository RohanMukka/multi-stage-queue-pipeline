Idea is to take in our generated synthetic workloads and visual the number of jobs and the time they arrive.

Examples for the plots and histograms can be found in the class provdied slides for the lab.

Input: wl.csv in the format generated by the workload generator.
Output: Plot and Histogram showcasing the amount of jobs and the time they arrive at.

Note: Output can be saved to file manually or automatically by the program it shouldn't matter.
