- The idea with this is that we have a plotting program that has two modes hist and scatter.

Hist:
- We take in the emetric.csv and a arguement specifying which field we want plotted.
- for example it could be ./plot-derive-event-metrics inputfile.csv output.png --hist="arrival-time"
- with that input we would plot a histogram of arrival time for every row present

Scat:
- We take in the emetric.csv and two arguments specifying the name of two columns you want plotted vs eachother
- for example it could be ./plot-derive-event-metrics inputfile.csv output.png --scat="arrival-time,response-time"

- style of plots can be seen in the slides
- typical matplotlib is fine here
