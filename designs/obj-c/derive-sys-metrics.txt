The idea here is that we take in the event log and track various statistics related to jobs.
- This should be for all jobs in our system not just one queue at a time. 
- It should generate time-series data the format of which is specified by the slide deck

Input: job.log which contains every event corresponding to every job in our system

Compute: all at a specific time step
- Average response time
- Median response time
- 10th percentile response time
- 90th percentile response time
- throughput (jobs in system at the timestep)

It is important all of this is to do with jobs that exit at the specific timestep we are looking at

Output: sysmetric.csv format specified in slide deck and example in examples/obj-c
