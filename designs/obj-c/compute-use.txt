The idea here is to take in a queue-met.csv and compute lots of statistics.

Things to compute:
- Average utilization
- Average saturation
- Total errors seen

These will all be computed over different time scales (10,100,1000) time steps

Utilization is the % of time a job is in the server
Saturation is the number of jobs queueing
Errors is the rate of errors that are originating in that queue

Input- qmet.csv 
Example- python3 qmet.csv


Example output:
- a queue use metrics file that can be put into a plotting program.
- The output format can be seen on slide 18 of the provided presentation.
